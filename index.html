01THE BUILDING BLOCK

One small unit.
Remarkable range.

An arithmetic logic unit is a processor’s decision point. It receives two values, follows an operation, and returns the result—one bit at a time.

Here, four switches make the whole process visible. Change an input. Change the operation. Watch the answer take shape.
